# Requesting a New Laptop or Hardware

Use the IT service catalogue for new laptops, monitors, docks, and approved peripherals. A hardware request needs the employee name, manager, department, office or delivery location, and a short explanation of the work requirement. Standard equipment follows the current catalogue; non-standard equipment requires an additional security and budget review.

1. Open the IT service catalogue from the Okta portal.
2. Select Hardware request and choose the closest catalogue item.
3. Enter the delivery location and explain the software or workload that requires it.
4. Add the manager as the approver and submit the request.
5. Track approval and delivery under My requests.

IT support confirms the asset record and may arrange collection of replaced equipment. Do not ship company hardware to a personal address unless the request has been approved for remote delivery. For a broken device that prevents work, open an incident instead of a replacement request and include the asset tag, symptoms, and a callback method.
