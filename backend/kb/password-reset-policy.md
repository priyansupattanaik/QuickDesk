# Password Reset & Account Lockout Policy

Employees must reset company passwords through the Okta portal. IT support cannot ask for or view an existing password. A password must be at least 12 characters and must not reuse a recent company password or contain the employee's name, email address, or a common phrase.

1. Open the Okta portal and select “Need help signing in?”
2. Choose “Forgot password” and verify using the registered MFA method.
3. Set a new password that meets the Okta requirements.
4. Sign in again to confirm the reset completed.

Five unsuccessful sign-in attempts lock the account for 30 minutes. Do not keep retrying during the lockout period. If MFA is unavailable, the account remains locked, or the recovery email is not accessible, contact IT support through the internal support form. Include your employee ID and a callback number, but never include a password, one-time code, or full payment information.
