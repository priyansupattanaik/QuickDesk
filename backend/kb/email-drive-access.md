# Requesting Email Groups & Shared Drive Access

Request access through the IT service catalogue so ownership and approvals are recorded. Email groups and shared drives are granted by the resource owner, not by an individual requester. Access should match the employee’s job duties and must be removed when the assignment ends.

1. Open the IT service catalogue from the Okta portal.
2. Choose Email group access or Shared drive access.
3. Enter the exact group or drive name, requested access level, business purpose, and requested end date if temporary.
4. Submit the request with the resource owner or manager listed as approver.
5. Test access after approval by signing out and back in if the resource does not appear immediately.

For a shared drive, request the lowest level that supports the work: viewer for reading, contributor for editing, and owner only for designated administrators. IT support can investigate a missing group or a permission error after approval. Do not share files by forwarding sensitive documents to a personal account while access is pending.
