# Expense Reimbursement Process

Submit work expenses through the ERP portal within 30 days of the transaction. Expenses must have a business purpose and should use the company payment method when one is available. Personal purchases require an itemised receipt and manager approval before reimbursement.

1. Open the ERP portal and choose Expenses, then New claim.
2. Enter the transaction date, amount, currency, cost centre, and business purpose.
3. Upload a legible itemised receipt; a card slip alone is not sufficient.
4. Select the correct expense category and add the project or client reference if required.
5. Submit the claim and monitor its status under My claims.

The manager reviews the business purpose first, then Finance checks policy and accounting details. Finance may return a claim for clarification rather than rejecting it. If a receipt is unavailable, attach the approved missing-receipt declaration when the ERP portal offers that option. Contact Finance support with the claim number for payment timing questions. Do not send card numbers or bank details in a ticket description.
