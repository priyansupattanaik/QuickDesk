# Setting Up the Company VPN

The company VPN protects traffic when you work outside the office and is required for internal tools that are not public. Install the approved GlobalProtect client from the IT software catalogue. Do not download a client from a search result or use a personal VPN for company work.

1. Open the Okta portal and sign in with your company account and multi-factor authentication.
2. Open the IT software catalogue, choose GlobalProtect, and install the version for your operating system.
3. Launch GlobalProtect and enter `vpn.quickdesk.internal` as the portal address.
4. Sign in through the Okta window and approve the MFA prompt.
5. Confirm that the client says Connected before opening the ERP portal or shared drives.

If the client repeatedly disconnects, record the time, network type, and exact error message. IT support can reset the VPN profile or check whether the gateway is having an incident. Never email your password or MFA code to support.
